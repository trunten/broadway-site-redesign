Leadership at Broadway

Broadway’s leadership structure brings together school improvement, curriculum, pastoral care, safeguarding, staff development and operational leadership.

The Academy continues to review and refine its curriculum and wider provision, with Heads of Department playing a significant role in shaping curriculum experiences. Current leadership work includes the Broadway Academy Living Curriculum and ongoing development across teaching, support and student experience.
