What SEND means

SEND means special educational needs and disabilities.

Broadway Academy wants every pupil to be included, learn well and feel safe.

We look at what each pupil is good at and what support they need.

People who can help

Razia Ali is the Director of SEND. Lulu Weston works in SEN Administration.

The SEND Department also has specialist teachers, Higher Level Teaching Assistants and Learning Support Assistants.

Support staff work closely with pupils, families, teachers and other professionals.

How we know a pupil may need help

We use information from primary school, teachers, parents and carers, pupils, assessments and outside professionals.

We use a cycle called Assess, Plan, Do, Review.

This means we keep checking that the support is helping.

Support in lessons

Most pupils are supported first through high-quality teaching.

Teachers may use clear instructions, smaller chunks of information, visual support, modelling, extra processing time and scaffolding.

Some pupils may also have small-group, literacy, numeracy, communication, nurture, mentoring or pastoral support.

Mental health and wellbeing

Pupils can ask for help when they need it.

Support can include trusted adults, quiet spaces, mentoring, nurture, Early Help, mental health services and safeguarding support.

We want pupils to feel listened to and supported.

Help in exams

Some pupils can have exam access arrangements when there is evidence that they need them and this matches their normal way of working.

Examples include extra time, a reader, reading pen, scribe, word processor, rest breaks, a prompter or a different room.

The school follows JCQ rules when deciding arrangements.

Parents, carers and pupil voice

Parents and carers are part of SEND reviews and can contact the SEND Team when they have concerns.

Pupils share their views through reviews, conferences, surveys and informal conversations.

Pupils should help shape the support they receive.

Moving to a new school, college or adult life

We support the move from primary school to Broadway Academy.

We also support Year 11 pupils moving to college, sixth form or other post-16 education.

Preparation for adulthood includes independence, communication, careers, employability and self-advocacy.

If you are worried

First, speak to the Form Tutor or Head of Year.

You can then contact the Director of SEND.

If your concern is not resolved, you can use the Academy Complaints Policy.

The four areas of SEND

Different pupils need different kinds of help

Communication and interaction

This can include autism and speech, language or communication needs.

Cognition and learning

This can include dyslexia, dyscalculia, dyspraxia and learning difficulties.

Social, emotional and mental health

This can include ADHD, ADD, anxiety and emotional regulation needs.

Sensory and physical needs

This can include hearing, vision, physical disability and medical needs.
