SEND information, support, official guidance and an Easy Read companion version.

01 Our Vision for SEND

Broadway is committed to a fully inclusive learning environment where every child can thrive academically, socially and emotionally.

02 Our SEND Team

Director of SEND: Razia Ali. SEN Administration: Lulu Weston. The department includes specialist teachers, HLTAs and LSAs.

03 Types of SEND We Support

Communication and Interaction; Cognition and Learning; Social, Emotional and Mental Health; Sensory and Physical Needs.

04 How We Identify Pupils

Identification uses transition information, teacher observations, parent/carer concerns, student voice, assessment, attendance/behaviour information and external reports.

05 SEN Support

Most needs are met through high-quality teaching, with targeted intervention and pastoral or alternative provision where appropriate.

06 SEND Registers

The Academy uses EHCP, SEN Support, Monitoring and Reasonable Adjustments as part of its graduated system.

07 How We Measure Impact

Impact is reviewed through academic, attendance and behaviour data, voice, intervention outcomes, learning walks, reviews and feedback.

08 Mental Health & Wellbeing

Support can include pastoral intervention, nurture, trusted adults, quiet spaces, mentoring and external mental-health services.

09 Exam Access Arrangements

Arrangements follow JCQ regulations and are based on evidence of need and normal way of working.

10 Working with Parents & Carers

Parents are involved through SEND reviews, annual reviews, calls, evenings, forums, surgeries and consultation.

11 Student Voice

Young people are central to decision-making and help shape the support they receive.

12 Specialist Services

Broadway works with Educational Psychology, autism and communication services, health professionals, Early Help and other specialists.

13 Preparation for Adulthood

Preparation focuses on independence, employability, communication, self-advocacy, community participation, further education and careers.

14 Transition Arrangements

Enhanced transition is available both into Year 7 and from Year 11 into post-16 provision.

15 SEND Developments 2026–27

2026–27 priorities include improving outcomes, adaptive teaching, parent co-production, student conferences, nurture pathways and staff expertise.

16 Complaints

Concerns should progress from Form Tutor / Head of Year to Director of SEND, then through the Academy Complaints Policy.

17 Further Information

The report signposts Birmingham Local Offer, SENDIASS, IPSEA, Forward Thinking Birmingham and NSPCC.
