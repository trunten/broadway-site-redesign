Our Vision for SEND

At Broadway Academy we are committed to creating a fully inclusive learning environment where every child can thrive academically, socially and emotionally.

We believe that SEND is a whole-school responsibility. Through high-quality teaching, effective partnerships with families and targeted support, we strive to remove barriers to learning and ensure that all pupils achieve their full potential.

Our SEND provision is underpinned by the principles of:

  • Inclusion for all
  • High aspirations
  • Equality of opportunity
  • Person-centred planning
  • Preparation for adulthood
  • Collaboration with parents and external agencies
We recognise and celebrate the strengths of every learner whilst providing the support required to meet individual needs.

SEND Team

The SEND Department is staffed by a highly skilled team comprising two Specialist SEND Teachers, two Higher Level Teaching Assistants (HLTAs) and six Learning Support Assistants (LSAs). To ensure support is responsive and closely aligned to students' needs, support staff are attached to specific year groups, enabling them to build strong relationships with students, families and teaching staff.

In addition, members of the team lead on identified areas of SEND need, providing expertise and guidance across the academy. These areas include Communication and Interaction, Cognition and Learning, Social, Emotional and Mental Health (SEMH), and Sensory and/or Physical Needs. This structure ensures that students benefit from targeted specialist knowledge whilst maintaining consistent support throughout their school journey.

The SEND Department works closely with

  • Senior Leadership Team
  • Heads of Year
  • Pastoral Team
  • Safeguarding Team
  • Teaching Assistants
  • External professionals

to ensure a coordinated approach to support.

What Types of SEND Do We Support?

Broadway Academy supports pupils across the four areas of need outlined in the SEND Code of Practice:

Communication and Interaction

  • Autism Spectrum Condition (ASC)
  • Speech, Language and Communication Needs (SLCN)

Cognition and Learning

  • Dyslexia
  • Dyscalculia
  • Dyspraxia
  • Moderate Learning Difficulties
  • Specific Learning Difficulties

Social, Emotional and Mental Health

  • ADHD
  • ADD
  • Anxiety
  • Emotional Regulation Needs
  • SEMH difficulties

Sensory and Physical Needs

  • Hearing Impairment
  • Visual Impairment
  • Physical Disabilities
  • Medical Needs

SEN Support at Broadway Academy

The majority of pupils' needs are met through High Quality Teaching.

Teachers are responsible for adapting learning through

  • Explicit instruction
  • Chunking information
  • Visual supports
  • Pre-teaching vocabulary
  • Modelling
  • Adaptive teaching strategies
  • Scaffolding
  • Additional processing time
  • Appropriate seating plans

Pupils may also receive

  • Small group intervention
  • Literacy support
  • Numeracy support
  • Social communication intervention
  • Nurture provision
  • Mentoring
  • Pastoral support
  • Alternative pathways
06

SEND Registers

Broadway Academy operates a graduated system of support.

EHCPPupils with an Education Health and Care Plan receive provision identified within Section F of their EHCP and participate in annual reviews.
SEN SupportPupils requiring provision additional to and different from their peers. Some pupils with complex SEND may receive enhanced support and personalised planning.
MonitoringPupils causing concern may be monitored whilst further assessment is completed.
Reasonable AdjustmentsSome pupils may require adjustments under the Equality Act 2010 even if they do not meet SEND criteria.

09

Exam Access Arrangements

Access arrangements are awarded in accordance with Joint Council for Qualifications (JCQ) regulations.

Arrangements may include:

  • 25% Extra Time
  • Reader
  • Reading Pen
  • Scribe
  • Word Processor
  • Rest Breaks
  • Prompter
  • Alternative Rooming
Arrangements are only awarded where there is evidence of need and a history of normal way of working.

SEND Developments 2026–27

During 2026–27 Broadway Academy will focus on:

Priority 1Improving outcomes for pupils with SEND across all year groups.
Priority 2Embedding high-quality adaptive teaching and Quality First Teaching.
Priority 3Strengthening co-production through a SEND Parent Forum.
Priority 4Developing Student SEND Conferences to increase pupil voice.
Priority 5Further developing nurture and alternative pathway provision at Key Stage 3.
Priority 6Increasing staff SEND expertise through targeted CPD and coaching.
