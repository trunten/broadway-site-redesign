Personal Development Programme

PDP develops the knowledge, skills and attributes students need to stay healthy and safe, build strong relationships and prepare for life and work.

The programme brings together PSHE, citizenship, careers and relationships and sex education. It is taught through dedicated form-time lessons and reinforced through subjects, assemblies and wider events.

Core themes

Health & wellbeing

Mental health, risk management, self-awareness, confidence and healthy choices.

Relationships

Families, respectful relationships, equality, bullying, racism and stereotypes.

Living in the wider world

Citizenship, careers, money management, target setting and preparation for adulthood.
