Careers education, information, advice and guidance

Broadway aims to give every student the knowledge, confidence, experience and impartial guidance they need to make informed choices about their future.

Careers learning runs from Year 7 to Year 13 through lessons, assemblies, employer encounters, educational visits, workshops and one-to-one guidance. Broadway works with Birmingham Careers Service and provides specialist SEND careers support.

The eight Gatsby Benchmarks

  1. A stable careers programme
  2. Learning from career and labour-market information
  3. Addressing the needs of each pupil
  4. Linking curriculum learning to careers
  5. Encounters with employers and employees
  6. Experiences of workplaces
  7. Encounters with further and higher education
  8. Personal guidance

Careers mission

Raise aspiration, build confidence, provide excellent impartial guidance and prepare students for successful transitions into further study, apprenticeships and employment.
