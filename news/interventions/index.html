Interventions & additional support.

How Broadway adds targeted and specialist support when students need more than universal classroom provision.

Home/News And Dates/Interventions

A graduated response to support

Broadway’s starting point is high-quality classroom teaching. Where a student needs more, support is planned through an assess–plan–do–review cycle and can increase in intensity according to need.

1

High-quality teaching

Adapted teaching, explicit instruction, chunked information, visual support, modelling, scaffolding, processing time and appropriate classroom adjustments.

2

School-based additional support

Targeted small-group or individual work, extra literacy or numeracy support, specialist equipment, mentoring, nurture, social-communication work and pastoral intervention.

3

External and specialist support

Where appropriate, Broadway works with services such as Educational Psychology, Speech and Language Therapy, sensory and physical-disability services, Occupational Therapy, Physiotherapy, mental-health services and other specialist providers.

Reviewing impact

Support is reviewed regularly using progress, attendance, behaviour, student and parent voice, intervention outcomes and professional feedback. Provision is adjusted when outcomes are not being achieved.
