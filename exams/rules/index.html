Preparing for examinations

Good revision is planned, manageable and consistent. Broadway’s exam guidance is designed to help students feel prepared rather than overwhelmed.

Planning revision

  • Create a realistic revision plan and include breaks.
  • Break subjects into manageable topics.
  • Prioritise areas that need the most attention.
  • Use practice questions and active recall rather than passive rereading.
  • Ask teachers for help early if something is unclear.
Remember

Your free time is not simply being given up — revision is an investment in your future.
