Collecting examination results

Results-day arrangements change each year, so dates and times should always be checked against the latest school communication.

If a student cannot collect results personally, the school requires advance consent before another person can collect on their behalf, and the nominated person must bring photo identification.

Staff are available on results day to support students with questions, next steps and Sixth Form enrolment.
