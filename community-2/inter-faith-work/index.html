Inter-faith and community cohesion

Broadway’s community work aims to help students understand their own beliefs, learn about the beliefs of others and develop the mutual respect needed to live well in a diverse city.

The Academy’s wider equality objectives include promoting cultural understanding and awareness of different religious beliefs, and Broadway has described an ambition to be a national leader and ambassador for inter-faith work.

Learning

Students encounter different faith traditions through the curriculum, assemblies and wider personal-development work.

Dialogue

Partnerships with faith communities create opportunities to ask questions, listen to others and challenge stereotypes.

Community

Broadway works with local groups and community partners to strengthen understanding across Perry Barr and Birmingham.

Faith visits and shared experiences

Broadway has used visits and local faith walks to give students first-hand experience of places of worship and the communities around them. These experiences support the Academy’s wider work on British Values, equality and community cohesion.
