Facilities and booking

The Community Centre offers spaces for sport, meetings, classes, events and celebrations. Charges vary by facility, time and booking requirements, so the centre confirms the current rate when a booking is made.

Sport

Sports hall, gymnasium, Astro pitch and other activity spaces.

Groups and classes

Classrooms, work areas, dance/drama space and conference facilities.

Events

Main hall and other flexible spaces for functions, celebrations and community events.

What affects the price?

  • The room or facility required.
  • The day and length of the booking.
  • Whether the booking runs beyond normal Community Centre opening hours.
  • Additional spaces or facilities, such as kitchen use.
  • The type and scale of event.
Get a current quote

Call the Community Centre on 0121 566 4345. The team can confirm availability, the current charge and any booking conditions.
