Fitness at the Community Centre

Broadway’s Community Centre includes a dedicated fitness suite as part of its wider sport and community facilities.

Fitness suite

The Academy’s facilities page describes a well-equipped fitness suite alongside the sports hall, gymnasium, Astro pitch and outdoor games areas.

Community sessions

The Community Centre programme includes Academy-run activity such as Xcite and Ladies Only gym sessions, alongside provision delivered by other community organisations.

Opening and membership

Session times, membership arrangements and availability can change with the Community Centre programme. Contact the centre before travelling to confirm the current timetable.

Fitness suite enquiries

Call 0121 566 4345 for current session and membership information.
