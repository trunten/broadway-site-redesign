Broadway Academy Community Centre

The Community Centre provides flexible facilities for sport, drama, dance, workshops, meetings, celebrations, faith groups and local activities.

The centre works with tens of thousands of community users each year. Facilities include the sports hall, main hall, gymnasium, Astro pitch, classrooms, drama and dance space, fitness suite and conference facilities.
