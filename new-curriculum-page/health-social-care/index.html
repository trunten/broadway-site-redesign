Why study Health & Social Care?

Health & Social Care gives students a practical and academic understanding of human development, care services, health and wellbeing.

Curriculum at Broadway

The BTEC Tech Award introduces the knowledge and professional values used across health and social care. Students learn how people develop, how services support them and how health and wellbeing can be assessed and improved.

Component 1

Human Lifespan Development explores physical, intellectual, emotional and social development across life stages and the factors that affect growth and development.

Component 2

Students investigate health and social care services and the values that underpin effective care, including meeting individual needs, communication and empowering service users.

Component 3

Health and Wellbeing asks students to interpret health indicators and lifestyle data, understand positive and negative influences, and create realistic improvement plans with targets and appropriate support.

Assessment and progression

The current BTEC Tech Award combines internally assessed components with an externally assessed component. Work is graded using Pass, Merit, Distinction and Distinction* standards.

Where this can lead

The course supports progression towards nursing, social work, physiotherapy, radiography, occupational therapy, speech and language therapy, teaching, early years and a wide range of care professions.
