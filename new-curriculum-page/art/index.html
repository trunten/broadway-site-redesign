Why study Art & Design?

Art & Design develops creativity, visual communication, practical skill and confidence in responding to artists, ideas and the world around us.

Curriculum at Broadway

Broadway’s Art curriculum is built around making, observing and responding. Students develop the technical vocabulary and practical habits needed to work increasingly independently, while learning how art reflects and shapes culture and history.

Year 7

The foundation year focuses on the basic elements of art: tone, colour, pattern, line, form, shape and texture. Students build observation and recording skills and learn strategies they can reuse in later projects.

Years 8–9

Students extend drawing, recording and making skills through increasingly sustained projects, responding to artists and experimenting with a wider range of materials, techniques and ideas.

Key Stage 4

GCSE work develops a personal portfolio through sustained investigations and practical outcomes. The externally set task follows the same assessment objectives and concludes with a supervised final outcome.

Assessment and progression

Assessment looks at how well students develop ideas, record observations, experiment with materials and techniques, and present a personal response. Regular sketchbook and practical work builds towards the GCSE assessment objectives.

Where this can lead

Art supports routes into fine art, illustration, graphic design, fashion, architecture, photography, animation, games design and the wider creative industries.
