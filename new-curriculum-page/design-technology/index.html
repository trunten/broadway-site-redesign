Why study Design Technology?

Design Technology is a practical and creative subject in which students design and make solutions to real problems, drawing on mathematics, science, engineering, computing and art.

Curriculum journey

Key Stage 3

Build core knowledge, vocabulary and subject-specific skills through a carefully sequenced curriculum.

Key Stage 4

Develop depth, independence and examination or vocational skills where the subject is offered as a qualification.

Key Stage 5

Continue into advanced study where the subject forms part of Broadway’s Sixth Form offer.

Assessment and progression

Students are assessed through the methods appropriate to the subject, with feedback used to identify strengths and next steps.
