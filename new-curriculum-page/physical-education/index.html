Why study Physical Education?

Physical Education develops sporting skill, tactical understanding, leadership and knowledge of healthy, active lifestyles.

Curriculum at Broadway

Broadway combines traditional and non-traditional sport with competition, leadership and co-curricular participation. The curriculum is designed to be inclusive while giving students clear progression into Key Stage 4 qualifications and Sixth Form sport.

Year 7

Students develop and describe core skills across football, rugby, basketball, badminton, fitness, netball, table tennis, trampolining, outdoor adventurous activity, rounders, cricket, softball and athletics.

Year 8

Students consolidate skills, add tactical understanding and connect practical work to components of fitness and GCSE terminology while continuing inter-form and inter-school competition.

Year 9

Leadership, tactical awareness and selecting and applying appropriate skills become more prominent, with practical activities linked explicitly to Key Stage 4 sport concepts.

Years 10–11

Core PE continues with invasion games, net/wall, fitness, striking and fielding, athletics and leadership. The option route includes OCR Sport Studies.

Sixth Form

BTEC Level 3 Sport includes anatomy and physiology, fitness training and programming, professional development in the sports industry and fitness testing, alongside Broadway’s sports academies.

Assessment and progression

Practical performance and leadership are combined with subject knowledge. OCR Sport Studies includes externally assessed contemporary issues plus centre-assessed practical and vocational units.

Where this can lead

Progression includes teaching, coaching, physiotherapy, sport and exercise science, personal training, performance analysis, sports management and leisure.
