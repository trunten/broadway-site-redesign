Drama at Broadway

Drama provides a broad, balanced and ambitious curriculum that develops performance skills, theatrical knowledge, creativity, collaboration and self-confidence.

Students learn how physical and vocal choices create meaning, devise original work, interpret scripts and explore different theatrical styles. At Key Stage 3, assessment draws on three areas: Head, Hands and Heart — thinking and decision-making, performance skills, and engagement and teamwork.

Progression

Key Stage 3

Performance technique, devising, character, genre, script work and theatre styles.

Key Stage 4

Vocational Performing Arts study through the Drama pathway, combining practical performance and creative development.
