Mathematics at Broadway

Mathematics develops analytical thinking, reasoning and efficient problem-solving — skills used across science, technology, finance and everyday decision-making.

Curriculum at Broadway

Broadway’s curriculum revisits and extends the connected areas of number, algebra, geometry, ratio and proportion, statistics and probability. Students are expected not only to carry out methods but to explain reasoning and apply mathematics in unfamiliar contexts.

Year 7

Number and algebra build on KS2 before ratio, proportion and geometry become a greater focus. Later work applies core ideas to equations, graphs, perimeter and area.

Year 8

Students extend prime factors, expressions and formulae, 3D volume, compound measures, non-linear graphs and probability, with continued emphasis on real-world applications and problem solving.

Year 9

Topics include fractional and negative indices, standard form, non-linear sequences, cumulative frequency, complex percentage problems and polygon angles, with STEM connections.

Years 10–11

GCSE work consolidates prior learning and extends into higher-order algebra, functions, iterative formulae, trigonometry, histograms and bounds before Year 11 concentrates on examination problem-solving and targeted intervention.

Sixth Form

A Level Mathematics develops algebra, coordinate geometry, trigonometry, vectors, calculus, exponentials and logarithms alongside mathematical applications.

Assessment and progression

Regular assessment is used to review understanding and identify gaps. At GCSE, students build fluency and reasoning alongside the communication needed for multi-step problems; Sixth Form develops increasingly formal mathematical proof and modelling.

Where this can lead

Mathematics underpins engineering, physics, computing, economics, finance, data science, medicine, architecture and many other degree, apprenticeship and employment routes.
