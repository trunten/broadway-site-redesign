Why study Business Studies?

Business Studies helps students understand how organisations operate, make decisions and respond to customers, finance, competition and change.

Curriculum at Broadway

Students examine businesses they encounter every day and learn how enterprise, people, finance, marketing and operations work together. The subject develops commercial awareness alongside decision-making, numeracy and extended written reasoning.

Year 10

AQA GCSE Business begins with Business in the Real World, external influences, operations and human resources. Students explore enterprise and entrepreneurship, how businesses respond to change, production and stock, technology and how organisations recruit, train and motivate people.

Year 11

The course moves into marketing and finance: market research, the marketing mix, sources and uses of finance, accounts and interpreting business performance. Students then bring the whole course together for the final examinations.

Sixth Form

A Level Business extends the same themes through deeper analysis of organisations, markets, strategy, finance and decision-making, using case studies and data to build evaluative arguments.

Assessment and progression

The current AQA GCSE is assessed by two equally weighted written papers. Questions include multiple choice, calculations, short answers, data response and extended evaluation.

Where this can lead

Business can lead towards accountancy, banking and finance, marketing, management, retail, administration, entrepreneurship and a wide range of university and apprenticeship routes.
