Our Food

As Broadway staff will run our kitchen, we can focus on fresh balanced meals that fuel learning, energy, and well-being—because we know what our students love and need.

Our (Healthy) Choice

With no outside company involved, it means no more “one-size-fits-all” menus! Students can help shape our meal choices with seasonal themes, taste tests, and fun food days.

Our Kitchen

As Broadway Academy will be running the kitchen we can keep meal prices fair and make sure every penny goes back into supporting students, not external profit. Health food at the right price.

We Will Be Serving Food Grown By Our Students On Our Farm.

Since September 2025, we’ve brought our catering in-house – which means fresher food, more student choice, and a school lunch that works for everyone.

Our fresh fruit and vegetables, grown by students at the Broadway Academy Farm, are served throughout the year as they come into season. Food from the farm can make its way directly into the Broadway kitchen.

Our staff prepare, cook and serve the food to our students. No outside catering company is involved.

The Right Food At The Right Time

Breakfast

Broadway Dining is excited to partner with Magic Breakfast to make sure every student starts their day with a healthy and tasty breakfast. A good breakfast helps students feel awake, focused and ready for the school day.

Learn more about Magic Breakfast (opens in a new tab)

Break Time

Perfect for keeping hungry teens going until lunchtime, our light snacks hit the spot and keep students satisfied.

Lunch

All our students can enjoy a great value meal deal at lunchtime—whether they’re entitled to Free School Meals or not. Each deal includes the dish of the day, a dessert, and a refreshing squash. All our meals are freshly prepared in-house by our dedicated team.

Food Choice

Our menus are designed to offer choice, take into consideration vegetarian, cultural and religious dietary requirements and meet the requirements of the Government’s food-based and nutrient-based standards for school lunches.

Fresh Food Locally Sourced

We’re passionate about serving fresh, high-quality food that’s cooked right here in our kitchen. By sourcing ingredients locally wherever possible and focusing on seasonal produce at its freshest, we make sure only the very best goes into every meal we serve.

A Different Menu

We listen to students and staff to fine-tune our menus. Ideas for new dishes, themed menu days and events celebrating different styles and cultures of food are welcome.

Available Daily

Filled Jacket Potatoes, Assorted Snacks, Various Filled Sandwiches, Rolls & Wraps, Fresh Salads. Hot Sweet of the Day or Fresh Fruit Salad, Hot and Cold Drinks.
