Broadway Dining: fresh food, student choice and an in-house kitchen.

Home/Student life/Broadway Dining

Since September 2025, Broadway has brought catering in-house: our own staff prepare, cook and serve the food, giving the Academy more control over freshness, choice and value.

Our Food

With Broadway staff running the kitchen, the focus is on fresh, balanced meals that fuel learning, energy and wellbeing — shaped around what our students need and enjoy.

Our (Healthy) Choice

Students can help shape meal choices through feedback, seasonal themes, taste tests and food days rather than relying on a one-size-fits-all menu.

Our Kitchen

Running the kitchen ourselves helps us keep prices fair and means money from the service supports Broadway students rather than an external catering company.

Food grown by our students

Fresh fruit and vegetables grown by students through Broadway’s farm and outdoor education work can be used as they come into season, linking growing, learning and the school kitchen.

Smart tools for parents

New tills use facial recognition to make service quicker and reduce till problems. Through the Arbor Parent App, families can:

  • see what their child has eaten;
  • top up the meal account online; and
  • keep payments cash-free.

The right food at the right time

Breakfast

Broadway works with Magic Breakfast so students can start the day with breakfast before lessons. The current food policy lists breakfast service from 8.10am to 8.35am, with options such as toast, crumpets and cereals.

Break time

Break-time food is designed to keep students going until lunch, with a mix of hot and cold snacks. Examples in the current food policy include pizza muffins, wholemeal toast, baked waffles, fresh fruit pots, homemade traybakes and sandwiches.

Lunch

Lunch includes a main meal option alongside choices such as Curry Club, Pasta King, wraps, jacket potatoes with salad and grab-and-go food. The menu operates on a three-week cycle with meat, fish and vegetarian choices.

Food choice and dietary requirements

Menus are designed to provide choice while taking account of vegetarian, cultural and religious dietary requirements and the Government’s food-based and nutrient-based standards for school lunches. Families should contact the Academy about medical allergies or other specific dietary needs.

Fresh food and seasonal produce

Broadway aims to use fresh, high-quality ingredients, source locally where possible and make use of seasonal produce. Student and staff feedback helps the kitchen refine menus and plan themed days and special events.
