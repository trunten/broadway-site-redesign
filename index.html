Welcome to Broadway

Our Children. Our Community. Believe it can be done.

Broadway Academy is an inclusive school serving Perry Barr and the wider Birmingham community. Our aim is simple: students should feel safe, happy, ambitious and ready for life.

We combine academic ambition with exceptional pastoral care and a rich wider curriculum. Students are encouraged to live the Academy values, develop their character and make the most of opportunities in lessons, sport, music, leadership, outdoor education and the community.

Our vision

We prepare students for life.

Broadway’s vision joins academic achievement with character, inclusion, wellbeing and service to the community.

Our wider offer includes extensive sport, arts and music, outdoor education at Island Pool and learning connected to Esperance Farm. We want students to leave Broadway with the knowledge, confidence, resilience and optimism to flourish.
