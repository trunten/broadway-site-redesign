How Broadway uses personal data

Broadway Academy Trust is a data controller and processes information about students, parents and carers, staff, governors, volunteers, visitors and other people who interact with the Academy.

Personal data is used to provide education and associated services, meet statutory duties, support safeguarding and employment responsibilities, and operate the Academy securely.

Data protection responsibilities

Governing Board

Holds overall responsibility for ensuring the Academy complies with its data-protection obligations.

Headteacher and CEO

Acts as the representative of the data controller on a day-to-day basis.

Data Protection Officer

The DPO service is provided by SIPS Education and can be contacted at gdpr@sips.co.uk or 0121 296 3000.

Data Protection Leads

Named Academy leads support student, staff, IT-systems and governor data responsibilities.

Your information rights

UK data-protection law provides rights over personal information, including access to information held about you and rights relating to accuracy, use and retention in applicable circumstances.
