Cookies on this website

Cookies are small files that websites can place on a device to support essential functions, remember choices and, where used, help understand how a site is being used.

Types of cookies

Essential

Needed for core website functions and security. These cannot normally be switched off through a cookie-preference tool.

Preference

Used where a site needs to remember a choice, such as a display or accessibility preference.

Analytics

Can help a website owner understand which pages are used and where improvements may be needed.

Embedded services

Video, maps or other third-party content may set their own cookies when those services are used.

Managing cookies

You can remove or block cookies using your browser settings. Blocking some cookies can affect how parts of a website work. Where optional cookies are used, a production version of the site should provide an appropriate consent control.
