Applying for Year 7

Applications for Year 7 are coordinated by Birmingham City Council. Families apply through the Council’s online admissions system and can name Broadway Academy as one of their preferences.

Birmingham online applicationSchool admissions guidance

Approved oversubscription criteria

Where there are more applications than places, the current arrangements give priority in this order:

  1. Looked-after and previously looked-after children, including IAPLAC.
  2. Children with a sibling already at Broadway who will still attend when the child starts.
  3. Children of eligible Broadway employees.
  4. Children living nearest the Academy.

Within a category, priority is decided by straight-line distance between the home address and Broadway Academy. Children whose Education, Health and Care Plan names Broadway are admitted through the EHCP process and sit outside the oversubscription criteria.

In-year admissions

Applications outside the normal Year 7 admissions round are made directly to Broadway Academy. The Academy aims to give a written decision within 10 school days and must do so within 15 school days. If a place is refused, the decision includes the reason and information about the right of appeal.

Waiting lists

Waiting-list positions can move up or down as applications change. Applicants are ordered using the published admission criteria.

Appeals

School admission appeals are administered by Birmingham City Council and heard by an independent panel.

Sixth Form entry

Sixth Form applications are considered against the academic requirements for the chosen programme. The current minimum entry requirements are four GCSE grades at 4 or above plus two grades at 5 or above, including English and Maths for A-level pathways; vocational pathways have their own requirements, and individual subjects may set additional grades.
