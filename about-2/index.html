About Broadway

A school built around students and community

Broadway Academy serves Perry Barr with an inclusive “can do” culture and the belief that every young person can achieve.

Our school combines a strong academic curriculum with pastoral support, character development and a wide co-curricular programme. Sport, music, outdoor education, leadership and community partnerships sit alongside classroom learning because preparing students for life means more than examination results alone.

Our values

Integrity, respect, optimism, responsibility, appreciation, aspiration, generosity and inclusivity.
