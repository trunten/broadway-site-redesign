We prepare students for life

Broadway’s vision is to provide a rich, exciting and inclusive education that develops students academically, socially, culturally, physically and spiritually.

Our curriculum is deliberately wider than the classroom. Students can experience sport, music, drama, outdoor education, leadership, community partnerships and learning connected to Island Pool and Esperance Farm.

Strategic priorities 2024–2029

Exceptional educational experience

A rich and diverse curriculum, strong attendance and extensive co-curricular opportunities.

Excellent teaching and outcomes

Highly effective teaching, strong communication and literacy, and excellent outcomes across key stages.

Leadership and wellbeing

A culture of hope, professional development, coaching and sustainable leadership.

Character and behaviour

Strong relationships, Academy values, safeguarding and high expectations for learning.

Community cohesion

Partnerships with families, faith communities, businesses and organisations locally and beyond.
