Moving from primary to secondary

Broadway works with feeder primary schools and families to help Year 6 pupils feel confident and prepared for the move to secondary school.

Before transition

Primary-school liaison, information sharing and pre-mentoring support where needed.

Additional SEND transition

Workshops, visits and planning for pupils and families who need extra support.

Transition days

Students experience lessons, routines and the school environment before September.

Autumn term

Induction, mentoring, parent drop-ins and settling-in support continue after pupils start.
