Governing Board

Broadway’s Governing Board brings together parents, staff, community representatives and other governors to provide strategic oversight of the Academy.

Strategy

Set the Academy’s strategic direction and ensure its vision, values and priorities remain clear.

Accountability

Hold leaders to account for educational performance, safeguarding and the experience of students.

Resources

Oversee financial performance and make sure resources are used responsibly in support of the Academy’s aims.

Published governance information

For each academic year Broadway publishes membership, declared business or pecuniary interests, committee information, attendance and downloadable governance data.

Current membership

The Board includes the Headteacher/CEO as an ex-officio member alongside appointed governors drawn from the local community and other stakeholder groups. Business and pecuniary interests and other governing-board memberships are published alongside member details.

Interested in governance?

If you would like to know more about the Governing Board or are interested in becoming a governor, contact the Academy through the school office.
