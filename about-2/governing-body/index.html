Governing Board

Broadway’s Governing Board works with senior leaders to set strategic direction, hold leaders to account and oversee the use of school resources.

Membership includes parents, staff, community representatives and governors appointed through the Academy’s governance arrangements.

Key functions

  • Set the aims and objectives of the Academy.
  • Agree policies and strategic priorities.
  • Monitor progress towards agreed objectives.
  • Oversee financial performance and use of resources.
  • Support and challenge the Headteacher and senior leaders.
