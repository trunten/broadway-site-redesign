Working with parents and carers

Broadway values open, two-way communication with families and a strong partnership around each student’s education and wellbeing.

Pastoral care

Students are supported through form tutors and dedicated year-group support teams. The pastoral structure is designed to help students feel known, supported and ready to learn.

Behaviour and expectations

Our approach is rooted in the Academy values and three simple expectations: Ready, Respectful and Safe. We aim to create a calm, orderly environment in which every student can learn and succeed.
