Student leadership

Students are given practical opportunities to lead, support others and shape Academy life.

Wellbeing

Student leaders support wellbeing, model positive behaviour and help create safe spaces for pupils.

Co-curricular

Leaders help strengthen clubs, trips and enrichment opportunities across the Academy.

Academic

Academic leaders work with departments and support pupils who need additional help in particular subjects.
