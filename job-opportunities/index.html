Work at Broadway

Broadway looks for colleagues who share its commitment to excellent learning, exceptional pastoral support and a rich co-curricular programme.

Staff development is a priority. The Academy describes a structured professional-development programme, leadership development linked to NPQs and development opportunities for teaching and support staff.

Professional growth

Structured CPD, mentoring and opportunities to build leadership and specialist expertise.

Wider experience

Work in a school with outdoor education, sport, music, community and enrichment opportunities alongside the academic curriculum.

Wellbeing

The recruitment information highlights employee support and wellbeing benefits alongside pension and development schemes.

Safer recruitment

Broadway Academy is committed to safeguarding and promoting the welfare of children and young people. Recruitment includes appropriate identity, qualification, reference, right-to-work and safeguarding checks, including an enhanced DBS check for successful candidates where required.

Current vacancies

Vacancies change frequently, so live job listings remain on the Academy’s dedicated recruitment service rather than being duplicated on a static page.
