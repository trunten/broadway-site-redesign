Learning without limits

Broadway Sixth Form combines academic ambition with character development, strong pastoral care and preparation for university, apprenticeships and employment.

Students are expected to develop independence, self-discipline and a confident “can do” attitude. High expectations for attendance, punctuality and progress sit alongside personalised guidance and a wide enrichment programme.

Academic study

A broad post-16 curriculum with pathways into higher education, training and employment.

Pastoral support

Dedicated Sixth Form staff, regular guidance and support with progress and next steps.

Enrichment

Volunteering, mentoring, Duke of Edinburgh, social action, sport and wider opportunities.

Higher education

UCAS support, university preparation and opportunities to explore professional pathways.
