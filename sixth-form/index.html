Bridging and inclusive pathways

The Bridging Course supports students who need a different route into post-16 learning. Provision can include Functional Skills English and Maths, digital skills and employability, helping students build confidence and prepare for further study, training or work.

Sports Centres of Excellence

Basketball

Broadway works with City of Birmingham Rockets to combine academic study with technical training, strength and conditioning, mentoring, performance review and competitive fixtures.

Football

A structured academy programme combines study with coaching, competitive matches, performance review and personal development.

Judo

Students can develop from beginner level, work towards a judo licence and access specialist coaching.

Explore Broadway’s Sports Academies.

Enrichment

Enrichment is an expected part of Sixth Form life. Options include formal qualifications such as EPQ and Core Maths alongside volunteering, mentoring, Duke of Edinburgh, sport, music, drama, enterprise, debate, societies and widening-participation programmes.

Sixth Form Bursary

The 16–19 Bursary can help eligible students with education-related costs such as travel, meals, books and equipment, university visits, trips and required clothing. Support includes the vulnerable-student bursary and a discretionary bursary assessed against household and personal circumstances.

Preparing for the next step

University

UCAS guidance, higher-education preparation and widening-participation opportunities help students make informed applications.

Careers and apprenticeships

Careers guidance, employer encounters and work-related learning are integrated into the wider Sixth Form programme.
