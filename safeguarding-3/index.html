Safeguarding at Broadway

Broadway Academy is committed to safeguarding and promoting the welfare of children and young people. Staff and volunteers are expected to share that commitment.

If you have a concern about the welfare of a student or member of staff, contact the school and ask to speak to a Designated Safeguarding Lead. Concerns can also be reported using the School SHARP system.
