The Broadway Seven

Seven commitments encourage students to make the most of school life inside and outside the classroom.

  1. Regularly attend co-curricular activities.
  2. Take part in educational visits, rewards and wider experiences.
  3. Use masterclasses, college and university opportunities.
  4. Complete work experience or another work-related learning opportunity.
  5. Live the Academy values every day.
  6. Reach your full potential in and beyond the classroom.
  7. Go above and beyond by volunteering when opportunities arise.
